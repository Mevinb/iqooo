# PATHWISE design direction

A thoughtful career companion for working adults who need their ambitions to fit around real responsibilities. The signature is a branching path: one starting point, several plausible futures, and a visible next step.

## Visual language

- **Surfaces:** pale green-white `#F5F8F7`, white `#FFFFFF`, mint `#DDEFE8`.
- **Hierarchy:** ink `#17342F`, deep green `#143F35`, primary teal `#116B5A`, secondary text `#62746D`.
- **Attention:** muted amber `#8B601B` on pale sand `#F4ECD9`; reserved for tradeoffs and funding gaps.
- **Typography:** bundled Plus Jakarta Sans, four weights. Strong sentence-case headings; short, reassuring copy; readable explanations without feasibility scores or invented precision.
- **Layout:** 24-point screen gutters, 48-point touch targets, a four-tab bottom bar, scrollable content, and persistent primary actions for the advisor, forms, and what-if preview.
- **Motion:** one brief fade when the conversation or scenario changes, disabled for reduced motion. No continuous animation is needed to imply an actual AI connection.

## Interaction principles

The person stays in control. Support is optional and described as learning preferences. Simulations are reversible until explicitly applied. Estimated costs and sample listings are labeled as such. Empty states suggest a useful action. Every primary control leads to a screen, a visible state change, or a clearly explained demo interaction.

The mobile app is the product. Desktop web wraps that same app in a presentation frame; it is not a separate marketing website or backend dashboard.
